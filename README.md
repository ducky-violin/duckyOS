DuckyOS!
<br><br>
You have been taken to a secret duck base, and you must prove your worth in order to join the Secret Duck Society. Will you take on the challenge?
<br><br>
Demo link: https://ducky-violin.github.io/duckyOS/
<br>

May you be the next Secret Duck Society member, and most importantly, HAVE FUN!

<details>
  <summary>Features</summary><br>
-Link to Wikipedia article with the Welcome Duck<br>
-Closeable welcome window<br>
-Openable welcome window (click the clock)<br>
-Draggable welcome window<br>
-Ducks that will hide if you click on them / drag them around (they’re shy! ;) )<br>
-Summon ducks back with the header text<br>
-Added 3 app icons; each opens a window or a notification/task<br>
-App icons will turn “selected” (become a circle) when clicked<br>
-Secret “keys” hidden throughout the page; you have to find all 3 in order to unlock the hidden door<br>
-Added alert() notifications to add variety to the types of messages the user gets<br>
-Also uses confirm() and a text input button for tasks<br>
-There is one task that has the prerequisite of completing the two other tasks to unlock<br>
-Secret door will give you a hint if you click on it twice and only if you have not completed one of the quests
</details>
